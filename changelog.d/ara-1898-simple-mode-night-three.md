### Fixed (Simple Mode)

- **Image this opens the plan sheet over a running plan** (#1898): from the sky or the Sky Album, Image this with a plan running now opens Plan's sheet to add the target to the queue. Before, it showed Plan with no sheet.
- **The guider hints name the row you can find** (#1898): the Guider, Calibration and Rig setup hints now say "Connect Camera (Guide) in Settings › Devices", the row that connects the guider. Settings › Devices has no "Guider" row. pt-BR also names "Ajustes › Dispositivos", the real section name.
- **The focus curve's axis labels no longer collide** (#1898): the position axis labels only its even steps, not the padded ends next to them.
- **The focus curve keeps one size for the whole autofocus run** (#1898): its axes show before the first probe, and the weak-fit warning and an error have their own slot under the chart instead of taking its room.

### Changed (Simple Mode)

- **The Guider panel has no status line of its own** (#1898): the status pill already says what the guider is doing, so the panel's top line that repeated it is gone.
